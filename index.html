Who We Are

A Digital Marketing student's answer to Phnom Penh's food waste problem.

Every day, restaurants and food businesses throw away leftover food, peels, and scraps that could become something useful. GE-Z is a web platform that lets restaurants schedule pickups the way they'd order a delivery — simple, fast, and part of a bigger cycle.

The waste we collect is processed into Smart Organic Cubes: compressed, pre-measured organic fertilizer that's easy for farmers and gardeners to use, wrapped in biodegradable packaging.

Our Story

Two problems, one fix

GE-Z is a Phnom Penh-based organic fertilizer startup, founded by a team of Digital Marketing students turning an academic SME project into a real business.

Over 51.9% of Phnom Penh's municipal waste is food waste, while Cambodia imports roughly 98% of its chemical fertilizer, with prices recently surging 25–30%. We collect food waste from BKK1 restaurants and residue from rural farms, and turn it into affordable, safer organic fertilizer — turning one problem into the fix for the other.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Mission

GE-Z connects city waste recovery with agricultural needs through three direct actions:

  • Shifting Urban Habits: Making waste sorting effortless for businesses so food scraps become a valued resource, not trash.
  • Empowering Farmers: Providing affordable organic fertilizer, like the Smart Organic Cube and Sack, that cuts costs and enriches soil health.
  • Closing the Loop: Connecting urban partners, rural communities, and local facilities to build a self-reliant agricultural system.

🌍 Vision

A clean, circular Cambodia where organic waste is valued and transformed into affordable local fertilizer rather than being sent to landfills, helping restore healthier soils and reduce dependence on imported chemical fertilizer.

💚 Core Values

  • Sustainability: Turning waste into value, not landfill.
  • Transparency: Fair terms with every partner, always.
  • Accessibility: Affordable fertilizer, real alternative to chemicals.

Our Products

Products & Services

From recovered waste to affordable organic fertilizer — what GE-Z makes, and how we source it.

Smart Organic Cube (SOC)

Fertilizer, Simplified

A pre-measured, compressed fertilizer cube wrapped in water-soluble paper and packed in a reusable tin. Dissolve in water and apply straight to the soil.

Dissolve → Mix into Soil → Plant Crops

Sack (Uncompressed Compost)

Fertilizer at Scale

Bulk, sterilized organic compost from the same recovered waste as SOC — sold single or wholesale for larger farms. Apply dry, worked into soil, or mixed with water.

Our Service

Organic Waste Collection

We recover waste from two supplier tiers and turn it into SOC and Sack.

Green Waste — Urban Businesses

Free pickup from BKK1 restaurants, cafés, and supermarkets. Partners get a Green Certificate, free bins, sorting guidance, and brand visibility.

Brown Waste — Rural Farms

We buy manure and crop residue from rural farms at a per-kilo rate, coordinated through village chiefs and our own collection team.

Our Roadmap

Our Journey

How we're building GE-Z, stage by stage.

IN PROGRESS

Stage 1 (Year 1) — Pilot Launch & Initial Commercial Sales

Onboarding our first 15-20 BKK1 restaurant and café partners, sourcing rural waste from Kampong Speu and Ang Snuol, and launching commercial sales of SOC and Sack to early customers.

UPCOMING

Stage 2 (Year 2) — Urban Expansion & The Tin Loop

Expanding collection into Toul Tompoung and Daun Penh, launching paid waste recovery at major produce markets, and rolling out our closed-loop tin exchange program.

UPCOMING

Stage 3 (Year 3) — Household Integration & Smart Logistics

Piloting residential waste sorting with local authorities, deploying Smart Box Stations in neighborhoods, and upgrading our logistics platform with real-time impact tracking.

UPCOMING

Stage 4 (Year 4-5) — Full Industrialization & National Reach

Commissioning GE-Z's own manufacturing facility and scaling distribution to major agricultural provinces across Cambodia.
