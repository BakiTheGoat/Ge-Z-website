Who We Are

A Digital Marketing student's answer to Phnom Penh's food waste problem.

Every day, restaurants and food businesses throw away leftover food, peels, and scraps that could become something useful. GE-Z is a web platform that lets restaurants schedule pickups the way they'd order a delivery — simple, fast, and part of a bigger cycle.

The waste we collect is processed into Smart Compost Cubes: compressed, pre-measured organic fertilizer that's easy for farmers and gardeners to use, wrapped in biodegradable packaging.

About GE-Z

Green Ecosystem, Zero Waste.

GE-Z sounds like "Gen Z" — because this idea comes from us, Gen Z. GE also echoes the Khmer word ជី. Green, Ecosystem, Zero Waste — a name for a generation building a circular economy in Cambodia, one restaurant at a time.

Logo Symbolism

What our mark means

The GE-Z logo represents our mission to transform food waste into a valuable resource through a circular economy.

The letter "G" stands for GE-Z and Green, symbolizing sustainability and environmental responsibility.

The circular design represents the continuous cycle of collecting food waste, converting it into compost, and returning nutrients to agriculture instead of sending waste to landfills.

The leaves symbolize growth, nature, and the renewal of resources.

The brown organic texture represents compost and healthy soil, while the golden grain pattern reflects agriculture and the value created from recovered food waste.

What We Do

Services & Products

From pickup to soil — everything GE-Z offers to restaurants, farmers, and gardeners.

Photo of collection route / bins

Waste Collection Service

Restaurants register as a "Food Waste Partner" on the GE-Z app and schedule pickups on their own timetable. Our team follows a set route, collects sorted organic waste, and transports it to our processing facility. Free bins and short staff training are included so sorting stays simple.

Photo of Smart Compost Cube

Smart Compost Cube

Collected waste is composted and compressed into the Smart Compost Cube — a pre-measured block of organic fertilizer, sized for a specific amount of land and water, and wrapped in biodegradable packaging. Sold to farmers, agricultural businesses, plant nurseries, and gardening stores.

Photo of certificate

Green Partner Program

Restaurant partners who consistently sort and hand over waste well receive a "Green Partner" certificate — recognition they can display and share, encouraging long-term participation in the program.
